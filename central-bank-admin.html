ISOLATED CONFIGURATION · LIVE TRACKER PROTECTED

Saving a draft or publishing this registry does not write to the tracker’s operational data, users, staff, active shift records or reporting archive. The running application will only consume these settings after a separate, tested integration and release.

SEPARATION OF RESPONSIBILITIES

Access & Rights stays separate

Account identities and the existing tier-permission matrix remain in ACCESS AND RIGHTS. This control room does not create users, edit PINs or duplicate the permission editor. Only ADMIN/DEV can open Access & Rights and change permissions for other users or tiers.
